## 1. Title and subtitle

**Primary**

- **Subtitle:** A Couples Gratitude Journal for Noticing Why

**Alternates**
1. **Tether: The 90-Day Couples Gratitude Journal — Write Apart, Reveal Together**
   *Tradeoff:* leads with the mechanic and the number, which is the stronger sales argument — but
   it's long, and KDP truncates subtitles in search results. Best if the cover carries "Write Apart,
   Reveal Together" as a band instead.
2. **Tether: A 90-Day Gratitude Journal for Two**
   *Tradeoff:* shortest and cleanest, reads well as a thumbnail — but drops "couples," which is the
   single highest-volume search word in this category. Not recommended.
3. **Tether: A Daily Gratitude Journal for Couples Who Want to Say Why**
   *Tradeoff:* keeps "daily" (a real differentiator against the weekly comps) and hints at the
   promise. Weaker than the primary on rhythm.

**Recommendation:** primary title, with "90 Days · Write Apart, Reveal Together" set as a line on
the cover rather than crammed into the subtitle field.

## 3. Book description (sales page)

> KDP accepts limited HTML. Limit 4,000 characters. The first two lines are all that shows before
> "Read more" — they carry the whole thing.

```
<b>You already know why you love them. Ninety days to say it out loud.</b>

<p>Most couples journals hand you both a page and ask you to fill it in together. Which means one of you writes first, and the other reads it, and agrees.</p>

<p>Tether has one rule that changes everything: <i>you don't read theirs until you've written yours.</i></p>

<p>Every day has two facing pages and one question. The left page is One. The right page is Two. Each of you keeps a side for the whole book. You answer without looking, and then you turn the book around and read them to each other — out loud, to their face.</p>

<p>That's when you find out what they noticed that you missed entirely.</p>

<h4>What's inside</h4>
<ul>
<li><b>90 daily prompts</b>, each one specific enough that you can't answer it in a word</li>
<li><b>13 themed weeks</b> that go somewhere — from noticing the ordinary, through repair and desire, to what's ahead</li>
<li><b>The Weekly Tether</b> — one shared question at the end of each week, done together, out loud</li>
<li><b>Undated</b>, so you can start on a Tuesday in March and miss four days without failing</li>
<li>Three minutes each. Some nights it's two lines. That counts.</li>
</ul>

<p>The prompts aren't the soft kind. <i>What is one small thing they did today that you almost didn't notice — but did? When did you catch them being kind to someone who wasn't watching? When did they love you at your least lovable?</i></p>

<p>Ninety days is long enough that you'll run out of the easy answers. That's the point. Around week three you'll have to actually look at them.</p>

<p>A keepsake worth opening in twenty years — your handwriting on the left, theirs on the right, and the exact record of what the two of you were paying attention to.</p>

<p><b>For anniversaries, weddings, Valentine's, or an ordinary Tuesday when you want to stop assuming they know.</b></p>
```

- **Character count:** ~1,620 / 4,000
- **The hook (shown before the fold):** *"You already know why you love them. Ninety days to say it
  out loud."* — followed by the "one of you writes first, and the other agrees" line, which is the
  recognition that makes the mechanic land.

**Short version (~50 words, for ads and social):**

> A couples gratitude journal with one rule: write your side without reading theirs, then read them
> to each other. 90 daily prompts across 13 themed weeks. Three minutes each. You'll find out what
> they noticed that you missed entirely.

## 8. Trim, paper, and cover

- **Trim:** 6 × 9 in
- **Paper:** **cream** — warmer, standard for prose and journals, easier to write on than bright white
- **Interior:** black & white
- **Bleed:** no (all content sits inside the margins)
- **Cover finish:** **matte** — a keepsake shouldn't show fingerprints

**Back-cover copy:**

**Spine:** `TETHER   ·   A Couples Gratitude Journal`

**Cover design brief** *(brief only — artwork is a separate deliverable)*
- **Feel:** quiet, warm, adult. A keepsake, not a novelty gift. It should look like something you'd
  leave out on a table.
- **Concept:** a single thin line — the tether — running across the cover, connecting two small
  marks. One thing, not a pattern. Let the white space do the work.
- **Type:** the title large in a warm serif; subtitle small; "Write Apart, Reveal Together" as a
  quiet band near the bottom.
- **Palette:** warm off-white ground, one muted accent (dusty terracotta or deep sage), ink-black
  type. Two colours total.
- **Avoid, and this matters:** hearts, infinity symbols, intertwined rings, watercolour florals,
  hand-lettered scripts, silhouetted couples at sunset. Every comp in the category uses at least
  two of these; not using any is itself a differentiator on a search results page.
- **Thumbnail test:** the title must be readable at 100px wide, and the single line must still read
  as one line.

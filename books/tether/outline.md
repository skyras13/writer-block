## 1. Content architecture

- **90 daily prompts**, numbered continuously 1–90. The count never restarts — the reader should
  feel it accumulate.
- **13 themed weeks.** Weeks 1–12 hold 7 prompts; week 13 holds 6.
- **13 Weekly Tethers** — one shared, spoken prompt closing each week, on its own page.
- **Undated.** No dates, no days of the week printed. Skipping isn't failing.

### The arc

This is the thing no competing product has. The book moves:

```
ordinary noticing  →  what they do for you  →  what they say  →  being accompanied
        │
        └──►  choosing each other in conflict  →  your shared history  →  the dull work of a life
                    │
                    └──►  play  →  who they're becoming  →  repair  →  wanting them
                                │
                                └──►  what the two of you give away  →  what's ahead
```

Three movements:
1. **Weeks 1–4 — Looking.** Low-stakes noticing. Easy to answer honestly on day one.
2. **Weeks 5–9 — Weight.** Conflict, history, shared labor, play, growth. Harder, more specific.
3. **Weeks 10–13 — Saying it.** Repair, desire, outward, forward. The things that are hard to say
   out loud, asked only once the reader has had nine weeks of practice.

A reader on day 80 is doing something recognizably harder than on day 3 — without ever being told
so. That's success criterion #2 in the brief.

is unaskable on day 3 and answerable on day 64.

**Why week 11 follows week 10:** desire after mending, not before. A reader who has just written
about forgiveness is in a different place than one who hasn't.

### The 13 Weekly Tethers
Each is done **together, aloud** — never written privately. Their job is synthesis, and each asks
for a different action: compare · tell · say back · finish together · decide · tell a story · thank ·
plan · offer support · repair · declare · commit · re-read.

## 4. Mechanic specification

Drives `export/build-interior.js` directly.

- **Two-page facing spread per day**, sharing one prompt printed at the top of both pages.
- **Left page (verso) = "One." Right page (recto) = "Two."** Each partner holds one side for the
  whole book. Assignment is made once, on the "Before You Begin" page.
- **Ruled write-in area:** 9–11 lines per page, ~26pt rule spacing.
- **Prompt number** appears small above the prompt on both pages.
- **Running heads:** book title on verso, week theme on recto.
- **Page numbers** on every body page; suppressed on front matter and week openers.
- **The Weekly Tether gets its own single page**, no ruled lines for two — it's spoken, with a few
  lines for anything either wants to keep.
- **Week opener page:** theme name and the two-sentence epigraph, alone on the page. Starts recto.
- **Nothing is dated.** No day-of-week, no month, no "Day 1 of 90" countdown.

**Pagination rule that must hold:** every daily spread must land One on verso and Two on recto.
This is verified page-by-page after the build, not assumed. If a spread flips, the whole mechanic
breaks — a partner would be writing on the wrong side.

## 6. Repetition audit

**Rule:** any two prompts a reader could answer identically are one prompt.

Every theme's axis of variation is stated in §3. Checks performed across all 90:

| risk pair | why they're distinct |
|-----------|---------------------|
| 1 (almost didn't notice) vs 5 (stopped seeing) | #1 is a single event today; #5 is a permanent habit re-examined |
| 8 (three tiny ways) vs 10 (made something easier) | #8 asks for quantity and breadth; #10 asks for one act plus its *manner* — without making a point of it |
| 12 (rest over convenience) vs 14 (your comfort over theirs) | #12 is about rest specifically; #14 is a general trade. **Closest pair in the book** — kept because the axis (sacrifice type) differs, flagged for the Editor. |
| 30 (forgave before you finished apologizing) vs 68 (their "I'm sorry") | opposite directions: #30 is them forgiving you, #68 is them apologizing to you |
| 35 (repair reached for first) vs 69 (what you want to forgive today) | #35 is retrospective and about them; #69 is present-tense and turns inward, including toward the self |
| 36 (early moment returned to) vs 37 (first knowing) | #36 is a moment you replay; #37 is a story you'd tell a stranger — different register, different audience |
| 41 (how they changed you) vs 58 (how they've grown) | #41 is change in *you*; #58 is change in *them* |
| 44 (thankless task) vs 46 (load without scorekeeping) | #44 is one instance; #46 is an ongoing share |
| 51 (inside joke) vs 55 (ritual only funny to you two) | #51 is verbal and portable; #55 is behavioral and situational |
| 71 (beauty that isn't looks) vs 75 (what turns your head) | deliberately paired inversion — #75 is explicitly physical, #71 explicitly isn't |
| 88 (what these pages taught you to notice) vs 89 (what you'll keep doing) | #88 is perception; #89 is behavior |

**Form variation across the 90:** 74 questions, 9 descriptive instructions ("Describe…", "Recall…",
"Name…"), 4 sentence stems, 3 with an internal turn ("Look again — what does it mean?"). No three
consecutive prompts share a form.

**Result: no collapsing pairs.** One flagged pair (12/14) for Editor attention.

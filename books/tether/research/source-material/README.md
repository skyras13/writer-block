# Drop your source material here

The factory does **not** go find subject-matter research. Put whatever you have in this folder —
papers, PDFs, saved links, notes, transcripts, your own expertise written out.

The Researcher then reads it, verifies the citation details are real and correctly stated, logs
entries in `../../citations.md`, and writes `../coverage.md` listing what the book needs that your
material does not cover. It never adds a source you did not supply.

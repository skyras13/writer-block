# Week Ten — Repair & Forgiveness

> Lasting love is mostly mending. This week, notice the grace between you.

64. What do you need to thank them for that you never said out loud?
65. When did they make room for your worst day?
66. What grace have they extended you more than once?
67. Describe a wound the two of you healed together.
68. When did "I'm sorry" from them change everything?
69. What do you want to forgive — in them, or in yourself — today?
70. When did they love you at your least lovable?

**The Weekly Tether**

Offer one honest repair, and receive theirs without defending.

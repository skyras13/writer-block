# Week One — Noticing the Ordinary

> Love mostly happens in the small print. This week, slow down enough to read it.

1. What is one small thing they did today that you almost didn't notice — but did?
2. Describe the exact sound of their laugh from this week. What set it off?
3. When did you catch them being kind to someone who wasn't watching?
4. What ordinary moment today would you be sad to lose?
5. Name a gesture of theirs so familiar you've stopped seeing it. Look again — what does it mean?
6. What did their face do the last time they were genuinely surprised?
7. Where were you standing the last time you thought, "I'm lucky"?

**The Weekly Tether**

Read this week aloud to each other. What did your partner notice that you missed entirely?

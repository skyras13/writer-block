# Week Six — Our Story

> You are a history no one else has lived. This week, turn a few pages back.

36. What early moment between you do you keep returning to?
37. When did you first know? Tell it the way you'd tell a stranger.
38. What has this person survived that makes you proud to stand beside them?
39. Name a season you got through together that you couldn't have alone.
40. What tradition — however small — belongs only to the two of you?
41. How have they changed you in a way you're grateful for?
42. What do you know now about love that you learned from them?

**The Weekly Tether**

Tell one story from your history the other didn't know you'd kept.

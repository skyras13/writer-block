# Week Eleven — Admiration & Desire

> You get to want the person you love. This week, say so.

71. What do you find beautiful about them that has nothing to do with looks?
72. When did you feel a rush of wanting them recently — and why?
73. What strength of theirs do you quietly rely on?
74. Finish this: "I still can't believe I get to…"
75. What about them turns your head after all this time?
76. When were you proud to be seen as theirs?
77. What would you fall for all over again?

**The Weekly Tether**

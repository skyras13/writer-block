# Week Two — Small Kindnesses

> The big gestures get remembered. This week, count the ones that don't.

8. Name three tiny ways they took care of you this week.
9. What did they hand you — literally or otherwise — before you had to ask?
10. When did they make something easier for you without making a point of it?
11. What comfort do they offer that you've quietly come to rely on?
12. Describe a time they put your rest above their own convenience.
13. What did they remember that you had forgotten?
14. When did they choose your comfort over their own?

**The Weekly Tether**

Which small kindness surprised you to be seen? Tell them so.

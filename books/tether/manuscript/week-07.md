# Week Seven — The Work of a Shared Life

> A life together is held up by a thousand dull, faithful acts. This week, name them.

43. What did they carry this week so you didn't have to?
44. Name a thankless task they did that quietly held your life together.
45. When did they handle something you were dreading?
46. What part of your shared load do they shoulder without keeping score?
47. Describe the last time "I'll take care of it" meant everything.
48. What did they fix, clean, or manage that you'd have left undone?
49. When did their reliability feel like tenderness?

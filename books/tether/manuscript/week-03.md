# Week Three — Words & Voice

> Some sentences we carry for years. This week, notice the ones worth keeping.

15. What is a sentence they've said to you that you've never forgotten?
16. When did their words land exactly when you needed them?
17. What do they call you that no one else does — and how does it feel?
18. Recall a time their honesty was a gift, even when it was hard to hear.
19. What did they say the last time you doubted yourself?
20. When were you grateful they stayed quiet instead of filling the silence?
21. What's a story they tell that you never tire of hearing?

**The Weekly Tether**

Say back to each other the words that mattered most this week.

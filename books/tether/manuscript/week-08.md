# Week Eight — Play & Laughter

> Joy is not the opposite of serious love. This week, notice the fun.

50. What made you both laugh until it hurt recently?
51. What inside joke still works no matter the mood?
52. When did they turn an ordinary evening into something fun?
53. What do you do together that makes you feel like kids again?
54. Recall the last time they were gloriously, unselfconsciously silly.
55. What ritual is only funny because it's the two of you?
56. When did their playfulness pull you out of a bad mood?
